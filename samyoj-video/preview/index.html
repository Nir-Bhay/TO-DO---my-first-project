The eight scenes

#SceneInLenWhat it has to prove
How this was made. No footage, no stock, no template — every pixel is drawn by film.mjs at the moment of the frame, then piped raw into ffmpeg at 1080p60. The soundtrack is lib/audio-synth.mjs: a 120 BPM bed and eleven UI sounds built from ffmpeg expressions, placed by the film's own cue list, so picture and sound cannot drift apart. The narration is the one piece that must be rendered on your machine — node voice.mjs --voice <id>.
