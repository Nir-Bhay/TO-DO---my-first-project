## How it was made

Every frame is drawn by code (`film.mjs` → `scenes.mjs`) and piped raw into ffmpeg at 1080p60 —
no stock, no footage, no editor. The soundtrack is synthesised in `lib/audio-synth.mjs` from
ffmpeg expressions: a 120 BPM bed plus eleven UI sounds, placed by this cue list. The narration
is rendered separately (`voice.mjs` for ElevenLabs, `piper-voice.mjs` offline) and placed on the
same grid, so either voice drops into the same mix.
