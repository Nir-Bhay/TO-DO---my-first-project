# Motion Engineering Reference & Research Audit — Velora Studio v3.0 (Deep 3D Visual Hook & Cinema Cursor Edition)

## 1. User Feedback Critique & Root-Cause Diagnosis
Review of v2.0 identified four high-impact creative opportunities:
1. **Weak Visual Opening Hook (`0.0s – 4.0s`)**: Opening with centered text on a dark grid lacked immediate visual imagery at Frame `0`. Viewers scrolling social feeds decide within `0.4s – 1.5s` based on **visual proof** (real Reel covers, UI cards, device frames, and visual tension), not plain text alone.
2. **Richer Product Storytelling (`More Content`)**: Expanding to **`30.0s` (`1,800 frames @ 60 FPS = 60 beats @ 120 BPM`)** across **7 fast-paced acts** allows showcasing **11 real `3200×1920` Retina Velora views** + **11 creator cover artworks**: Rapid Reel Collage Hook, AI Co-Pilot + 4:5 Safe Crop, Comment-to-0.8s Meta Rich DM on a 3D iPhone, Drag-and-Drop 4:5 Grid + Analytics, Priority CRM + Claude/Cursor 58-Tool MCP Agent Terminal, Live `With Data` / `Without Data` OAuth Laser Scan + 20 Free Tools, and an 8-Card Dual-Ring 3D Orbit Finale.
3. **Expressive Multi-State Cursor Choreography (`Cursor Movement`)**: Upgraded from a simple single-target arrow to **`CinemaCursorRig`** — multi-waypoint quadratic Bézier trajectories across every act, union-silhouette **Arrow / Pointing Hand / Grabbing Hand** morphing (`ashrafchowdury/programatic-demo`), 5-dot sapphire velocity trails, banking rotation, dual concentric `96px` click rings, tactile audio foley (`sfx-swoosh.m4a`, `sfx-tap.m4a`, `sfx-click.m4a`), and Screen-Studio camera zoom coupling (`>= 56px` label clearance at rest).
4. **Deeper Atmospheric Theme & True Multi-Plane 3D Z-Depth (`Deepness & Visual Richness`)**: Every act now renders four true depth planes — **Plane 0** (3D `rotateX(64deg)` perspective horizon grid + sapphire/cyan volumetric lighting + vignette), **Plane 1** (blurred background bokeh UI/cover cards in deep Z-space), **Plane 2** (elevated 3D `StudioWindow3D` with rim lighting & dual contact/ambient shadows), and **Plane 3** (foreground 3D iPhone frame, MCP terminal, bezier data-impulse wires, keycap HUDs, and vector cursor).
